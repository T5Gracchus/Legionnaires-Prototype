Read the rules before deployment

Signature is measured from equipped weapons AND armor. Safe turns = encounter allowance − highest personal signature − one turn per 3 signature carried by the remaining squad (minimum 5). Equipping technology counts even if you never fire it. Your exact budget appears above.

At zero, enforcement appears and marks a recruit. You have two more player turns. At each turn end, the marked recruit is vaporized unless already extracted. At the end of the second turn, all remaining personnel are lost. Vaporized recruits cannot be revived. The native guards are vulnerable too.

Follow the mission checklist: hold ground, defend waves, guide a cart, destroy engines, or secure unlocked objectives. Then move onto an X extraction tile and use Extract for each recruit. Extracted units leave immediately. A mission can succeed with losses if all mission goals are complete and at least one recruit escapes. Aim to bring all four home.

Ground movement uses up/down/left/right at cost 1. Allies, rubble, trees, and water block ground paths. Flight and Leap can bypass them but must land on empty ground. X/Y are ground columns and rows; Z is height. Each recruit has a ground allowance, total Z travel, maximum upward step, and safe-drop distance. Cardinal steps cost 1; climbing also spends Z travel. Drops beyond the safe distance deal 3 HP per excess level, previewed before confirmation; lethal drops are unavailable. Flight ignores transit obstacles and drop damage but respects its landing height limit; Leap respects the leaper’s climb/drop limits. Weapon and interaction ranges count |ΔX| + |ΔY| + |ΔZ|. High terrain, trees, and rubble can block sight. Adjacent cover reduces ranged damage by 1 unless the shooter is higher; elevation adds no direct damage bonus.

Damage varies. Dodge avoids the hit; block reduces it by 2; armor and Defend reduce it further. Basic sidearms never run out. Revive an adjacent fallen recruit to 30% HP with Item/Special; they act next round. Musk reduces damage by 1 and dodge by 10 percentage points in radius 2 for two enemy phases. It affects allies too; Murr and filtered armor are immune.

Support enemies show intentions when inspected. Menders heal a living ally for 4 HP, twice per mission, with a two-round cooldown. Binders reduce Move by 1 and dodge by 10 percentage points through the next player turn and enemy phase; three uses and a two-round cooldown, no stacking. Callers grant nearby allies +1 damage for one enemy phase; two uses, a three-round cooldown, no extra attacks. Removing a support enemy prevents its next ability. Cooldowns and uses appear in tile inspection.

Pause play to take a break. Hidden-tab and manual-pause time are separate from estimated active time. Foreground inactivity beyond 60 seconds is estimated idle time. Saved results include full states, action/decision events, paths, combat rolls, and timing.

These missions reset their roster on deployment; trial losses do not permanently remove recruits. The Roman tutorial remains available separately.
